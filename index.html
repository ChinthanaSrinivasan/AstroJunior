Earth-based astronaut experiments

Real astronauts practise important skills on Earth before travelling into space. In this training stage, you will explore six learning activities: how underwater buoyancy helps astronauts practise controlled movement, how objects behave in microgravity, how to respond calmly to mission alerts, how to report a crew health concern, which life-support checks are important before a spacewalk, and why spacecraft systems must be inspected regularly. Each activity introduces a basic science or safety idea that helps astronauts prepare for the challenges of space.

LEARNING GOALUnderstand the science, teamwork and safety checks behind astronaut preparation.
